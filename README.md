# Korean Dessert Market Feasibility Research
## 韓式傳統甜點市場可行性研究

A data-driven study evaluating whether 개성주악 (Gaeseong Juak) 
can succeed as a specialty dessert shop in Seoul.

---

### Research Scope｜研究範圍
- Seoul: 72 benchmark Korean dessert shops (menu & review analysis)
- Overseas: 850 consumer reviews across Taipei, Tokyo, Vancouver
- Tools: Python · Apify · pandas · matplotlib · seaborn

### Key Finding｜核心發現
Consumer preferences for **nuts, low sweetness, and crispy texture** 
appear consistently across all 3 overseas cities — 
and 개성주악 naturally satisfies all three through its ingredients.

### Methodology Note｜方法論說明
This project uses **cross-shop keyword frequency** instead of total 
mention count to separate consumer demand signals from single-store 
supply effects — a correction identified during the research process.
